# Deep research in free mode

All report features use the configured zero-priced OpenRouter model. There are no
purchased credits, subscription gates or paid-model fallbacks.

## Pipeline

1. Gather up to twelve distinct pages, prioritizing pricing, customer stories, product
   details, security, integrations, documentation and comparisons.
2. Include up to three founder-supplied competitor roots before deeper company links.
3. Plan company-specific questions and run three bounded investigations.
4. Challenge the reasoning with counterarguments and missing evidence.
5. Synthesize the report, strategic insights and next experiments.

Sources include URLs, retrieval timestamps and excerpts. Existing SSRF, redirect,
response-size and timeout protections apply. Missing evidence must remain missing.
There is no general web search service. Marketing claims do not establish customer
demand or independent validation. Competitors and prospects may remain empty.

## Recovery and limits

Checkpointed investigation allocations are bounded to three passes. Explicit retry
uses completed checkpoints. Job leases, elapsed-time limits and one-active-report
controls remain in force.

The adapter requires zero-priced routing, disables paid fallback and search plugins,
rejects tools, and validates structured output locally. Rate limits produce a failure,
not a paid request. Failed previews do not consume the successful-preview allowance.

Fo retains its separate Wajo connection and approval controls.
See [FREE_MODE.md](FREE_MODE.md) for configuration and provider availability, and
[FREE_MODE_VERIFICATION.md](FREE_MODE_VERIFICATION.md) for verification evidence.


## Decision depth update, 2026-10-24

Every investigation, skeptical review and synthesis now uses seven decision lenses:
buying mechanics, alternatives, economics, distribution, causal explanations,
disconfirmation and decision value. Unsupported economics use explicit formulas or
labelled scenarios rather than invented ROI. Tests must specify an audience, measurable
signal, timebox and what changes if supported or rejected. Suggested thresholds remain
experiment design choices, not statistical proof.

Fetched sources carry up to 6,000 characters through research and synthesis. Source
inventories explicitly identify missing pages and limited coverage. Evidence is saved
in the investigation checkpoint and reused across passes and interrupted jobs.
Existing completed reports are preserved; generate a new report for the new pipeline.

Thirty-seven relevant backend regressions passed after this update. They cover access,
report lifecycle, checkpoints, crawl budgets, unavailable pages, content deduplication,
cross-origin navigation and source-only analysis. Ruff passed for changed Python files.
A bounded live provider check returned HTTP 429. Therefore the new pipeline has not
been validated with a complete live model-generated report. No paid fallback was used.
